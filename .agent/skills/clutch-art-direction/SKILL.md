name: clutch-art-direction
description: The single creative idea for Clutch Code. Use for layout, motion, imagery and iconography decisions.
---
Idea: "Everything clicks into place." The logo is two interlocking blades turning around one point. The site is a mechanism: separate pieces align, snap and lock.
Visual language:
- Rotation + snap: elements arrive slightly rotated/offset and settle into alignment (spring or stepped easing, never floaty fades)
- Registration marks, crop marks, alignment guides, measurement ticks, and tolerance lines as the decorative vocabulary (like an engineering / print-production sheet)
- Large blade/arc geometry derived from the logo used as cropped, oversized background forms (not glow)
- Two-piece compositions: split headlines, split panels that slide together on scroll or hover
- Numbers and specs shown as "readouts" (tabular, mono)
Colour: ink base stays. Introduce a warm off-white paper tone (#EDEBE4) for full-bleed contrast sections so the site alternates dark/light instead of staying dark. Indigo #5E5DE5 is for the "click" moment (the lock state, active, CTA). Add one sharp signal colour for status only (#C8FF3D lime) used very sparingly, max once per viewport.
Never use gradients as decoration; flat colour, hairlines, and texture (fine grain or halftone) only.
