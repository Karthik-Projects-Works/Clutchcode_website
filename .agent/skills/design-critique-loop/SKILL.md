---
name: design-critique-loop
description: Mandatory self-review loop after every page is built.
---
For each page: screenshot at 1440 / 820 / 390. Then write a critique in 5 bullets: (1) what is memorable, (2) what looks templated, (3) where hierarchy is weak, (4) where motion is gratuitous, (5) accessibility issues. Fix the top 3 issues and re-screenshot. Repeat once more. Present before/after in the walkthrough artifact. Be harsh; do not praise your own work.
