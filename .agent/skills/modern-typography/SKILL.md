name: modern-typography
description: Type scale, pairing and rules for a distinctive editorial-technical look.
---
- Replace Poppins as the display face with a characterful grotesk or serif display for headlines, loaded from Google Fonts or self-hosted: choose ONE of (a) "Space Grotesk"/"Familjen Grotesk" tight + large, (b) "Instrument Serif" for expressive italics mixed with a sans, (c) "Bricolage Grotesque". Pick one, justify it in the plan, never mix more than 2 families + mono.
- Body: a clean sans at 16–17px (e.g. "DM Sans" or "Instrument Sans"). Mono (JetBrains Mono) only for data, specs, labels.
- Scale: extreme contrast. Hero headline 9–14vw on desktop, line-height 0.9–1.0, letter-spacing -0.03 to -0.05em. Body stays small. Avoid mid-sized everything.
- Use variable font axes (weight, width/optical size) for hover and scroll-linked changes where supported.
- Mixed-style headlines: one word in italic serif or outlined text for emphasis (once per headline).
- Max line length 62ch. Tabular numerals for all figures.
