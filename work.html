What “one team” looks like in practice.

A selection of projects across software, marketing, and branding — each with the problem we started from and what changed.

SoftwareMarketing

Horizon Retail — Inventory dashboard and launch campaign

Challenge. Stock for three branches lived in separate spreadsheets, and nobody trusted the totals.

What we did. Built one internal dashboard, connected supplier data, then ran the launch campaign for the new ordering flow.

Weekly stock counts dropped from a full day to under an hour.
Branding

Greenleaf Organics — A brand identity for a regional organic grocer

Challenge. A growing grocer with three different logos across signage, bags, and social.

What we did. Defined the positioning, designed one identity system, and delivered a full brand book with print-ready files.

New identity rolled out across stores, packaging, and social in six weeks.
Marketing

Brightside Café — Local search and social growth

Challenge. Great coffee, but nobody outside the street knew the café existed.

What we did. Fixed the local listings, set up a weekly content rhythm, and ran small, measured paid campaigns.

Map views and walk-in bookings climbed steadily across the first three months.
Software

Northfield Clinic — Appointment booking portal

Challenge. Reception staff spent most of the day on the phone confirming slots.

What we did. Built a booking portal with reminders and a simple admin view for the front desk.

Phone confirmations fell sharply, and no-shows dropped after reminders went live.
BrandingSoftware

Atlas Hardware — Rebrand and catalogue website

Challenge. A 30-year-old trading name with a dated look and a paper catalogue.

What we did. Refreshed the identity, then built a searchable online catalogue in the new look.

Customers now browse and enquire online instead of phoning for prices.
SoftwareClutchKart

Greenfield Supermarkets — ClutchKart rollout across three stores

Challenge. Paper ledgers at the counter and no live view of stock between stores.

What we did. Set up ClutchKart, imported the product list, trained the billing staff, and supported go-live week.

One dashboard for all three stores, with low-stock alerts before shelves empty.
